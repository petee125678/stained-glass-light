# stained-glass-light
this is for my stained glass light!!
stained glass is a really long art process
where you first start by making or finding a pattern, for me i made a pattern online with pixlr
then after you stick your pattern on by either printing it and glueing the paper on or instead, printing it on sticker paper and using that
after that you cut your glass to match the pattern as much as you can using a special tool that has a diamond tip to score the glass then you either use pliers or use your hands to break it apart
then you use a glass grinder that also has a glass tip to make the edges not smooth and so the next step works out, you also do this to make your glass more acurate to your pattern so in he end everything fits better
after that you use copper foil that has a non sticky side and a sticky side which you put around your glass pieces induvidually so youre able solder the glass together, without this step you wont be able to the rest of these steps
then you solder it together by using flux and lead solder(lead solder works much better but there is also non lead solder which is usually used for stained glsss pieces that are going to be touching the skin alot or if you just want to be safer)
this is normally the final step unless you feel like you want to add some extra things like patina(which oxidises the solder in certain way so it can be diffrent colors like cooper, black, or bronze) or for some projects you want to add a chain to your piece so you can hang it up(this is where you do this)
